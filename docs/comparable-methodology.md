# Comparable methodology

The engine applies hard eligibility context first: levy code, property class,
homestead status, and assessor neighborhood when populated. It then applies
tolerances for living area (20%), market value (25%), year built (15 years),
bedrooms, and bathrooms. Candidates are ranked by weighted physical similarity
and the tax rate is deliberately excluded from that score.

The published summary uses the comparable median and 25th/75th percentile
range. Confidence is high at 15 or more eligible candidates, medium at 5–14,
and low below 5. This is informational analysis and not a determination that a
property is over- or under-taxed.
